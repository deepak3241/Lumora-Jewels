Our Story

A family workshop that grew into Jaipur's name for bridal jewellery

Lumora began in 2004 as a two-artisan workshop behind Jaipur's Panch Batti — commissioned pieces, mostly bridal, sold entirely by word of mouth. Two decades on, we still take every commission personally, and every regular customer's name is still on a first-name basis with someone at the counter.

Jewellery Care

Keeping a piece beautiful for the next fifty years

A little care goes a long way — store pieces separately, keep them away from perfume and chlorine, and bring gold and diamond pieces in for a professional polish once a year.

Learn More
  • CleaningWarm water and a soft brush, never harsh chemicals.
  • StorageKeep pieces separated to avoid scratching.
  • Avoiding ChemicalsRemove jewellery before perfume, chlorine or cleaning.
  • Diamond CareA soft toothbrush and mild soap keep the sparkle.
  • Gold CareWipe with a soft cloth after each wear.
  • Professional CleaningBring pieces in once a year for an inspection.
  • RepairsOur workshop handles resizing, re-plating and repairs.
